# Future Station

![Future Station](assets/fs-logo-primary.svg)

**Print-on-demand, marketed to millions — without paid ads.**

Future Station is a print-on-demand and digital marketing company based in Dhaka, Bangladesh, active since 2021. We design and sell print-on-demand products, market them organically to an audience network we built ourselves, and run content monetization across Facebook, TikTok, YouTube, and Instagram. Our audience is concentrated in the United States.

## Live site

- **Website:** https://hexfrodo-spec.github.io/future-station/
- **Portfolio:** https://hexfrodo-spec.github.io/future-station/portfolio.html

## Results

- **Active since 2021** — four years of continuous operation
- **$200–300K+** profit from free, organic marketing — zero paid ad spend
- **~$100–200K** profit from content monetization
- **~5–10M** audience presence in the USA across platforms
- **~4M** USA Facebook audience across 30+ pages and 30–35 groups
- **Team of 10** across design, marketing, and content production

## What we do

- **Print-on-demand design & production** — original designs, quality control, and multi-product catalogs across dozens of niches.
- **Organic audience marketing** — products marketed to our own pages and community groups; no paid advertising.
- **Content monetization** — original image and video content produced as a repeatable system for monetized pages.
