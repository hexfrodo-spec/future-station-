Who we are

Future Station is a print-on-demand and digital marketing company based in Dhaka, Bangladesh, active since 2021. It was founded and is led by Founder & CEO Saiful Islam Shimul, together with Co-founders Bijoy and Tuhin Patowary and a team of 10. We design and sell print-on-demand products, market them organically to audiences we built ourselves, and run content monetization across Facebook, TikTok, YouTube, and Instagram. Our audience is concentrated in the United States.

What we do

Print-on-demand design & production

Original designs, rigorous quality control, and multi-product catalogs across dozens of niches — apparel, drinkware, home goods, gifts, and seasonal lines. End-to-end POD operations: original design, quality control, transparent print-file preparation, and multi-product catalogs.

Organic audience marketing

We don't buy reach; we own it. Products are marketed directly to our own Facebook pages and community groups, with content distribution across TikTok, YouTube, and Instagram — giving partners distribution without an ad budget.

Content monetization

Original image and video content produced at scale for monetized social pages, run as a repeatable production system with AI-assisted workflows.
